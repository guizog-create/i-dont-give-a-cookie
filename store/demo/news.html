Researchers map the quiet coastline where seabirds return every spring

By A. Writer · 6 min read

For three decades, a small team of volunteers has counted the birds that come back to the same stretch of coast each April. Their notebooks now form one of the longest continuous records of its kind.

The data show colonies shifting slowly north, following the fish they feed on. "Every year tells us a little more," says one of the volunteers.

The team hopes the record will help planners decide where new protected areas are most needed.
